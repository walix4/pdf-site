The conditions

What we ask for

A pledge is not an account application

Opening an investment account in Pakistan from abroad means documents, verification, and a wait. Recording a pledge means eight fields. The difference is deliberate.

Requirements vary between institutions. Recording a pledge here does not open an account or begin any application, and the two are entirely independent of one another.
